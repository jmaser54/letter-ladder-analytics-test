Letter Ladder

Created by Jeff Maser

How to Play

Build a ladder by filling in the missing rungs. Each rung adds exactly one letter to the word below it — you can rearrange the letters however you like, as long as every letter carries up plus the new one.

  • Words can be guessed in any order
  • not could climb to tone, note, or torn
  • A letter can repeat — lead can become ladle by adding a second "L"
  • Every word in the ladder must be a real English word

There's usually more than one path to the top — how many can you find?

All puzzles handmade by Jeff Maser.

Dictionary adapted from the zeisler/scrabble project on GitHub.
